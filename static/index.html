📱 Renogy BT-1 Bluetooth Pairing & Field Diagnostic Guide

Step 1: Check BT-1 Power LED

The green POWER LED on the BT-1 module must be solidly lit. If unlit, verify the RJ12 cable is firmly clicked into the controller's RS232 port.

Step 2: Disconnect Renogy DC Home App

Renogy BT-1 only accepts one simultaneous Bluetooth connection. Close the official Renogy phone app completely (force-stop) before pairing here.

Step 3: Linux Daemon Watchdog

The backend runs an autonomous supervisor that executes bluetoothctl power on and restarts bluetooth.service automatically if connection freezes.
