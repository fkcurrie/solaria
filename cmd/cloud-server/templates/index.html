🔌 Safe Wiring Sequence & Commissioning Wizard

⚠️ Golden Rule of Solar Commissioning:

ALWAYS connect the 12V Battery Bank to the MPPT Controller BEFORE connecting solar panels. Connecting PV first without a battery reference voltage can permanently destroy internal buck converters.

Step 1: Connect 170Ah LiFePO4 Battery Bank MANDATORY 1ST

Connect battery (+) to Controller Battery (+) and (-) to Battery (-). Ensure 30A inline ANL fuse is installed. Controller LCD will power on.

Step 2: Connect Renogy RTS Temperature Sensor STEP 2

Plug 3.5mm RTS probe into the temperature port and tape copper sensor lug directly to the LiFePO4 casing. Arms sub-zero freeze protection (0°C cutoff).

Step 3: Connect Renogy BT-1 Bluetooth Module STEP 3

Plug RJ12 cable from BT-1 module into RS232 port of Rover MPPT. Green LED will flash to broadcast Bluetooth Modbus RTU telemetry.

Step 4: Solar Panel Orientation & 45° Pitch Tilt Alignment STEP 4

Align solar array facing South-East (135° Azimuth) at a 45° tilt angle. This orientation maximizes morning solar harvest over Wren Lake and sheds Muskoka lake-effect snow in winter.

Step 5: Wire 4x100W 2S2P Array & Close PV Breaker STEP 5

Verify MC4 branch Y-connectors (2 series pairs paralleled). Close 20A DC breaker. MPPT solar indicator illuminates and reads ~36-40V Voc.

Step 6: Verify Live Sync & Autonomous Guardian STEP 6

Check that Solaria dashboard displays real-time watts, battery SOC%, and green health badges.
