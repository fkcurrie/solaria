## 🌟 The Core Mission

The mission of the **Renogy BT-1 / BT-2 Hardware Emulator** is to provide a physical/virtual Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) peripheral written in **Go** that behaves with 100% fidelity like a genuine Renogy communication module wired to an off-grid solar installation.

It serves as the definitive testbench, safety sandbox, and chaos laboratory for off-grid solar engineers. Central clients (Web Bluetooth in Chrome, Raspberry Pi edge pollers, Python GATT scripts, and the official Renogy DC Home iOS/Android app) can discover, pair with, stream telemetry from, and write control commands to this emulator over real 2.4 GHz Bluetooth radio signals without requiring access to live physical high-current hardware.

## 🏛️ Guiding Architectural Tenets

### 1. Indistinguishable Byte-Level Fidelity
- **Exact GATT Topology:** Must broadcast the genuine Renogy Service UUIDs (`0xFFD0` serial communication, `0xFFF0` telemetry notification).
- **Exact Characteristic Handles:** Accept 8-byte Modbus RTU requests on `0xFFD1` and deliver sliced responses on `0xFFF1`.
- **Realistic UART Chunking:** Physical Renogy BT-1 modules bridge an RS-232 UART running at 9600 baud to BLE ATT packets. Telemetry responses (73 bytes) MUST be sliced into 20-byte chunks with an authentic **15ms - 25ms inter-packet delay** to accurately emulate hardware buffer latency.
- **Strict CRC-16 Adherence:** Checksums must strictly calculate over polynomial `0xA001` with zero tolerance for corrupted frame construction in nominal mode.

### 2. Physics-Based Determinism (No Fake Random Number Generators)
- **Solar Irradiance Fusion:** Telemetry is not generated by static random numbers. Solar voltage, current, and wattage must flow from continuous astronomical models (NOAA solar elevation $\alpha$, array azimuth $135^\circ$, tilt $45^\circ$, and localized cloud attenuation).
- **The Night Invariant:** When the sun is below the horizon ($\alpha \le 0^\circ$), solar wattage $P_{\text{pv}} \equiv 0\text{W}$ and current $I_{\text{pv}} \equiv 0.00\text{A}$. The emulator will NEVER generate phantom power in the dark.
- **2S2P String Dynamics:** The array electrical output respects the series-parallel curve ($V_{\text{mp}} \approx 36\text{V}$, $I_{\text{mp}} \approx 11\text{A}$, $V_{\text{oc}} \approx 44\text{V}$) with negative temperature derating ($-0.40\% / ^\circ\text{C}$).

### 3. Rigorous LiFePO4 Electrochemistry & Thermal Safety
- **True OCV Discharge Curve:** Emulates the flat voltage plateau ($13.1\text{V} - 13.3\text{V}$ between 20% and 90% SOC) and the steep low-knee drop-off below 20% SOC for a 12V 170Ah (2,176Wh) LiFePO4 bank.
- **Dynamic Internal Resistance ($R_{\text{int}}$):** Terminal voltage dynamically sags under high load and rises during bulk charge ($V_{\text{term}} = V_{\text{ocv}} + I \cdot R_{\text{int}}$).
- **Sub-Zero Lithium Inhibit (The Golden Safety Rule):** When temperature drops to $\le 0^\circ\text{C}$ ($32^\circ\text{F}$), the emulator unconditionally cuts charging current ($I_{\text{charge}} = 0\text{A}$) and trips fault register bitmask `0x0080` to prevent simulated lithium plating damage.

### 4. Multi-Device Partitioning & Scalability
- **Partitioned Identity:** A single host or BLE 5.0 controller can partition its radio stack into multiple distinct virtual peripherals (e.g. Rover 20A MPPT at Address `0x01` and 170Ah Smart Lithium Battery at Address `0xF7`).
- **Isolation:** Modbus write operations to one device node (such as turning off the load circuit on the controller) do not alter or corrupt the BMS cell voltage registers of the battery node.

### 5. Chaos as a First-Class Citizen
- **Fault Injection Sandbox:** The emulator includes an external control plane (REST/CLI) to safely simulate conditions that would be dangerous or impossible to test on live cottage batteries:
  - Blown bypass diodes (halving string voltage to 18V)
  - Severe tree canopy shading and cloud occlusion
  - Disconnected RTS temperature probes (floating ADC readings)
  - Battery low-voltage disconnect (LVD) collapsing under heavy inverter loads
  - Bluetooth RF signal degradation and silent packet drops

### 6. Pure Go (Golang) Implementation Invariant
- **Strict Go Language Standard:** The emulator MUST be written **exclusively in Go (Golang)** across all packages, CLI tools, and background services.
- **Zero Heavy Runtime Dependencies:** No Python interpreters, Node.js runtimes, or external C++ daemon dependencies.
- **Native Concurrency & Performance:** Leveraging Go goroutines, channels, atomic operations, and standard memory safety for microsecond-level Modbus dispatch, non-blocking D-Bus event loops, and multi-client connection scaling.
- **Single Static Binary Compilation:** The entire emulator suite compiles into a single, high-performance static binary runnable on Linux x86_64 and ARM64 (Raspberry Pi).
